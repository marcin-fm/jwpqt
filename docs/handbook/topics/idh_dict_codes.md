# Dictionary Codes

Definitions retain source metadata in parentheses. These tags identify grammar, register, orthography, or source classification; they are not translated by JWPqt and depend on the dictionary that supplied the entry. Common EDICT tags include `abbr` (abbreviation), `adj` (adjective), `adj-na` (adjectival noun), `adj-no` (noun taking `no`), `adj-pn` (pre-noun adjective), `adj-s` (special adjective), `adj-t` (`taru` adjective), `adv` (adverb), `arch` (archaism), `aux`/`aux-v` (auxiliary), `conj` (conjunction), `col` (colloquial), `exp` (expression), `id` (idiom), and `int` (interjection).

Other useful markers are `ek` (usually kanji), `uK` (usually kanji alone), `uk` (usually kana alone), `gikun` (meaning reading), `iK` (irregular kanji), `ik` (irregular kana), `io` (irregular okurigana), `oK` (outdated kanji), `ok` (outdated kana), `obs` (obsolete), `obsc` (obscure), `fam` (familiar), `pol` (polite), `hon` (honorific), `hum` (humble), `fem` (female language), `male` (male language), `m-sl` (manga slang), `MA` (martial arts), `sl` (slang), `vulg` (vulgar), and `X` (rude/X-rated).

Parts of speech include `n` (common noun), `n-adv` (adverbial noun), `n-t` (temporal noun), `n-suf` (noun suffix), `pref` (prefix), `suf` (suffix), `neg`/`neg-v` (negative usage), `qv` (see another entry), and `vs`/`vs-s` (suru noun/verb). Verb tags include `v1` (Ichidan), `v5` (unclassified Godan), `v5u`, `v5k`, `v5g`, `v5s`, `v5t`, `v5n`, `v5b`, `v5m`, and `v5r` for their Godan endings; `v5k-s` for iku/yuku; `v5z`, `v5aru`, and `v5uru` for special classes; `vk` for kuru; and `vi`/`vt` for intransitive/transitive.

ENAMDICT-style records can label `f` (female given name), `g` (unclassified given name), `m` (male given name), `p` (place name), `s` (surname), and `u` (unclassified person name). [Limiting the Search](help:IDH_DICT_LIMITS) can exclude the 21 selectable tagged-sense categories exactly, but it filters senses rather than trying to classify prose. A tag absent from that list remains display information. Classical records add their own grammar vocabulary; see [The Classical Japanese Dictionary](help:IDH_DICT_CLASSICAL).

The exclusion controls correspond to `vulg`, `X`, `col`, `m-sl`, `sl`, `MA`, `id`, `arch`, `obs`, `obsc`, `ok`, `abbr`, `fam`, `pol`, `hum`, `hon`, `fem`, `male`, `pref`, `suf`, and `oK`. They compare the source tag exactly, including case: `oK` and `ok` are different codes. Filtering is applied to a sense before accepted-result limits and fallback decisions, so it affects direct, pattern, inflection, contingent, and optional-name passes consistently. If a recognized group contains a selected excluded type, that sense is removed while allowed meanings in a mixed entry can remain. An unrecognized tag stops filtering the rest of that source group; it does not authorize guessing a category from its surrounding English text.
